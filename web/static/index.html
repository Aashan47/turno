How Turno decides

  
    
    
  1. Forecast. The ECMWF open-data ensemble, 10 members, at the site's 0.25 degree cell. Each member gives its own hourly heat stress (WBGT, Liljegren method via ECMWF thermofeel). ECMWF open data is 3-hourly; hours between are interpolated linearly.
    2. 
    
  2. Limits. NIOSH 2016-106: the Recommended Alert Limit for unacclimatized workers, the Recommended Exposure Limit for acclimatized ones, as 1-hour averages. An hour's work is the largest of 60, 45, 30, 15 or 0 minutes that keeps the hour within the limit, planned for the hard end of the workload range.
    3. 
    
  3. Cautious plan, human decision. The plan follows the hottest of the 10 members. When members are 30 minutes or more apart, the hour goes to the supervisor with the reason. Settings were chosen on June 2026 dates only and tested on July and August.
    4. 
    
  4. Law. The regional ordinance bans work 12:30 to 16:00 on days Worklimate rates "ALTO". Turno never guesses that rating: the supervisor confirms it. Until then the ban is assumed.
    5. 
    
  5. Models. Models on AMD (Token Factory) read each region's ordinance PDF and explain hand-offs; Gemma 4 writes the briefing. Neither decides anything: every value they write is checked against the plan or the source text.
