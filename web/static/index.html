How Turno decides

The forecast. ECMWF's ensemble, ten members, at the site's grid cell. Each member gives its own heat stress for every hour, as WBGT by the Liljegren method (ECMWF thermofeel).

The limit. NIOSH 2016-106: the Recommended Alert Limit for crews new to the heat, the Recommended Exposure Limit for acclimatized crews, as one-hour averages. An hour's work is the most of 60, 45, 30, 15 or 0 minutes that keeps the hour inside the limit, planned for the hard end of the work range.

The plan follows the hottest member. When members are 30 minutes of work or more apart, the hour goes to the supervisor with the reason. These two settings were chosen on June 2026 days only, then tested on July and August.

The law. The regional ordinance bans work 12:30 to 16:00 on days Worklimate rates ALTO. Turno never guesses that rating. Until the supervisor confirms it, the ban is assumed.

The models. Models on AMD read the ordinances and explain hand-offs; Gemma 4 writes the briefing. None of them decides anything, and every number they write is checked against the plan or the source text.
